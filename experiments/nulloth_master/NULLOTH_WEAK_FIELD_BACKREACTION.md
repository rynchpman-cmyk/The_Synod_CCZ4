# The Weak-Field Backreaction of Nulloth

## Synodic question

Campaign `NUL-GRV-001` compares a growing mode-one coherence seed of amplitude
(0.02) with an independently constraint-solved scalar-free control.  The pair
is repeated at gravitational couplings (5\times10^{-4}) and (10^{-3}) under
the same harmonic gauge, material state, carrier modulation, grid, and time
step.

The campaign distinguishes two effects.  The initial physical-metric contrast
records the geometry required to satisfy the constraints in the presence of
coherence stress.  The dynamical contrast subtracts each member's initial
geometry before taking the active-versus-control difference, and therefore
records subsequent geometric response rather than merely the initial solve.

## Standard of judgment

A weak-field backreaction observation requires a nonzero physical-metric
contrast, a nonzero dynamical contrast, and an approximately linear response
when the gravitational coupling is doubled.  Material and carrier number,
complete-step entropy, CCZ4 and field constraints, constitutive convexity, and
primitive recovery remain independent vetoes.

## Boundary of the Article

These one-dimensional periodic trajectories do not carry a qualified
transverse-traceless radiation channel.  The campaign therefore measures
weak-field backreaction only.  It does not claim gravitational radiation,
gauge invariance, an asymptotic flux, or a global gravitational energy.
