# The Twelve-Unit Coherence Trial of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-NLN-006` extends the small growing seed from the six-unit regime
atlas to twelve units of complete production evolution.  The six-unit state
was classified as sustained coherence because it had not yet turned over.
The extended trajectory is required to determine whether that classification
represented an attractor or merely a phase of a longer nonlinear orbit.

The registered judgment is `nonlinear-turnover` if the scalar peak grows by
the declared factor and the endpoint lies at least five percent below that
peak.  Coherence lifetime is reported only as a lower bound when the endpoint
amplitude remains above (0.06).

## Scope

This Article can revoke an asymptotic interpretation of the six-unit state,
but it cannot identify the final decay endpoint.  The four-point periodic run
is not a converged long-time solution, and its coordinate energy is governed
by the separate geometric-work account.  Turbulence, collapse, finite
boundaries, and true metastable decay remain open.
