# The Reserves Do Not Add

Status: **the registered joint targeting corner is denied**

## Judgment

Independent targeting tolerances are not additive permissions. More sharply,
the earlier positive second-harmonic result was not generic shape-noise
robustness: reversing that harmonic changes the sign of its benefit.

```text
positive 10 percent harmonic alone      4.18251 percent   pass
negative 10 percent harmonic alone      1.53251 percent   fail
phase bound plus low amplitude           1.90447 percent   fail
phase bound plus momentum underdrive     2.07279 percent   pass
phase bound plus negative shape          0.64399 percent   fail
all registered detrimental errors        0.64362 percent   fail
```

The equal-magnitude shape signs differ by `2.65` percentage points of
suppression. A single deterministic harmonic realization shall therefore not
be named stochastic-noise qualification.

## Reserve accounting

At the qualified `19.6875`-degree phase bound, twenty-percent momentum
underdrive still clears the objective, but only narrowly. Ten-percent low
amplitude does not. Negative shape error dominates both its phase pair and the
all-error corner.

The all-error result cannot be interpreted as a pure nonlinear interaction
penalty because negative shape already fails in isolation. It does establish
that spending the independent phase, amplitude, shape, and momentum allowances
together is forbidden for a two-percent request.

## Safety classification

Every trajectory remained admissible. Maximum number drift was `3.08e-16`,
minimum entropy increase was `2.37e-2`, maximum gravitational-constraint L2
was `1.29e-6`, and maximum recovery residual was `3.30e-11`. These are safe
objective failures.

The Synod shall realign or weaken the request. It shall not declare a
constitutive emergency solely because one of these combined trajectories
misses two-percent suppression.

## Next writ

The detrimental shape magnitude must be bracketed, and stochastic noise must
be treated as an ensemble with multiple phases and realizations. Only then can
a probabilistic noise reserve be combined with phase, amplitude, and momentum
budgets. Physical latency and damaged routes remain separate finite-domain
campaigns.
