# The Vector and Material Pulse Trial of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-GRV-005` advances two independent three-dimensional sources on
dynamical spacetime.  The vector trial uses a transverse mixed-Proca momentum
mode, (\pi_A^y=10^{-3}\cos kx), whose divergence vanishes identically.  The
material trial uses a two-percent baryon-density mode.  Each source and the
uniform-material, vector-free control receive independent periodic CMC
constraint solves.

The source amplitudes are measured by their complex Fourier coefficients,
not by extrema.  Geometry is compared active-versus-control both on the
initial slice and after subtracting the initial geometry from each evolved
state.  A Fourier transverse-traceless projection separately records the
extrinsic-curvature response.

## Scope

This trial establishes that the real production initializer, recovery map,
mixed-Proca system, material system, and CCZ4 geometry can carry independent
vector and material modes without violating the registered vetoes.  It does
not establish propagation speed, dispersion, radiation, resolution
convergence, long-time survival, or finite-boundary behavior.
