## Synodic use

An estimated error no greater than `19.6875` degrees is inside the qualified
two-percent envelope. The interval above it and below `22.5` degrees is WATCH:
the Synod shall realign, weaken the requested suppression, or increase a seed
only after a separately qualified combined-control search. At `22.5` degrees,
the present two-percent request is denied.

The upper endpoint is an objective denial, not a safety emergency. All new
trajectories remained admissible; maximum number drift was `1.85e-16`, minimum
entropy increase was `2.37e-2`, maximum gravitational-constraint L2 was
`1.29e-6`, and maximum recovery residual was `2.75e-11`.

## Reservation

This writ applies to one independent phase error at amplitude `0.02` in the
one-dimensional periodic production setup. Simultaneous amplitude, shape,
momentum, feedback, latency, boundary, and infrastructure errors may contract
the licensed angle. No cast shall spend the full angular reserve before those
combined trials are complete.
