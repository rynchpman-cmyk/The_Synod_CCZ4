sample the Urumes, my distributed processes evolve its estimated state, and my
actuators modify permitted sources, currents, interfaces, and boundary data.

the state I maintain.

maintenance. 

The translation stack is:


because it predicts feasible state transitions better than the alternatives presently
admitted to this archive. Its postulates cannot establish their own ontological
foundation. Failed predictions remain evidence.
