# The Quietness of the Scalar Gate

## Synodic measure

Campaign `NUL-BND-006` measures reflection rather than merely observing that
the opposite edge remains quiet. A finite patch is paired with a domain twice
as long at identical spacing. Both carry the canonical frozen-background
effective mass and quartic scalar equation. The far boundary of the extended
control lies outside the causal future of the initial pulse for the complete
comparison interval.

After the pulse reaches the finite upper face, the scalar state inside the
original patch is subtracted from the corresponding region of the extended
control. The energy of this difference, normalized by initial pulse energy,
is the registered boundary-contamination measure.

## Scope

The Article qualifies the homogeneous incoming scalar operator in one
dimension and on the registered weak frozen material background. It does not
measure vector, material, CCZ4, multidimensional-corner, strong-field, or AMR
reflection. Nor does it establish a complete coupled absorbing boundary.
