# The Weak-Field CCZ4 Constraint Trial of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-GRV-003` advances the same active growing coherence mode on 4,
8, and 16 point periodic grids.  It reports the scalar amplitude and the CCZ4
constraint norms as separate convergence sequences.  Field constraints,
number conservation, entropy, constitutive convexity, and primitive recovery
remain independent vetoes.

The maximum Hamiltonian-or-momentum norm must decrease monotonically across
the resolution ladder.  The scalar endpoint must independently approach its
finest-grid value.  Neither sequence is permitted to stand in for the other.

## Scope

This is a short-horizon weak-field propagation trial in harmonic slicing.  It
does not establish long-time constraint damping, convergence in strong
curvature, radiation accuracy, finite-boundary behavior, or AMR consistency.
Those remain separate Articles.
