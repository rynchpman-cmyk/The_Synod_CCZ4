# Low-Resolution Production Regimes of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-NLN-003` advances four constraint-solved periodic states through
the complete `NullothProductionSolver` for six time units.  Material,
coherence, both mixed-Proca fields, cleaners, and CCZ4 geometry are evolved.
The carrier density has a twenty-percent mode-one modulation so that the
production drag channel is physically active and its entropy is measurable.

Three outcomes are observed:

- the exactly scalar-free control remains scalar free;
- the (0.005) growing-mode seed reaches sustained coherence without turning
  over before the horizon;
- the (0.02) growing seed and (0.06) preloaded seed reach nonlinear turning
  amplitudes and decline by more than ten percent before the horizon.

Every registered trajectory remains below the coherence-amplitude wall,
inside both convexity gates, and recoverable.  Baryon and carrier number drift
remain at roundoff.  Scalar and Gauss constraints remain at roundoff, while
the maximum CCZ4 Hamiltonian or momentum constraint is approximately
(1.21\times10^{-5}).

## Energy statement

The evolved `combined_energy` is the densitized material-plus-vector-plus-
scalar energy variable.  Its periodic integral changes by about five percent
because the constraint-solved geometry evolves and performs gravitational
work.  It is not a conserved global energy for this dynamical periodic
spacetime.  The campaign records this coordinate-energy change but does not
misidentify it as loss, gain, or spell work.  A gravitational energy and
radiation account belongs to the CCZ4 gauntlet.

## Entropy statement

Net material entropy and every complete-step entropy increment are positive.
The sum of complete-step increments telescopes to the final entropy change
exactly.  Carrier drag supplies an explicit positive contribution and vector
damping is disabled.

The manifold projection is also applied at internal Runge--Kutta stages.  Its
diagnostic sum is not an additive physical channel: the same provisional
entropy may be corrected at several stages and then discarded.  In this
campaign that stage sum exceeds the final entropy change.  Consequently the
campaign observes nondecrease and closes the complete-step telescope, but it
does not claim a fully decomposed physical entropy budget.

## Development boundary

Exploratory uniform-carrier versions triggered the strict entropy guard on
small negative manifold-projection increments.  No guard or continuum
equation was weakened.  The registered campaign instead uses the carrier
modulation already present in the qualified production spell, providing a
real dissipative channel.  A (0.12) coherence stress seed was separated from
this campaign because its recovery cost prevented the complete multi-regime
matrix from finishing inside the process window; it remains unclassified here.
