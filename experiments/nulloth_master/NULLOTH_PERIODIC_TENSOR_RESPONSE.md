# The Periodic Tensor Response of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-GRV-004` places an anisotropic coherence profile on a fully
three-dimensional periodic grid.  The profile is proportional to
(\cos kx-\cos ky) and is compared with an independently constraint-solved
scalar-free control.  Amplitudes (0.005) and (0.01) test the source scaling.

For every nonzero Fourier wavevector, the spatial metric and extrinsic
curvature contrasts are projected with the transverse-traceless projector.
The initial TT contrasts must be at roundoff because the CMC data are
conformally flat with no anisotropic momentum.  A subsequent nonzero TT
contrast therefore records tensor geometry generated during evolution.
Because canonical scalar anisotropic stress is quadratic in the scalar
amplitude, the low-to-high response ratio is predicted to be four.

## Radiation boundary

This is a periodic tensor-geometry source diagnostic, not an asymptotic
gravitational-wave measurement.  The production solver does not yet support
the nonperiodic vacuum extraction region required by the existing finite-
radius `Psi4Extractor`.  The Synod therefore records tensor excitation but
withholds the name gravitational radiation until resolution, propagation,
vacuum extraction, and boundary-contamination trials are complete.
