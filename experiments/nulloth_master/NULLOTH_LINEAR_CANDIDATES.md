# Register of Linear Nulloth Candidates

## Synodic status

The Synod records the following as candidate mechanisms.  None is, by this
record, a spell.  A spell requires a controlled nonlinear trajectory,
admissibility throughout that trajectory, an energy and entropy account, and
the promotion tests declared elsewhere in the workplan.

## Candidate L-1: coherence ignition window

Within the qualified five-dimensional state atlas, the local scalar curvature

\[
  M_\varphi^2=m_{\rm eff}^2+3\lambda_\varphi\varphi^2
\]

is negative in 16 of 832 sampled states and positive in 816.  The negative
curvature states provide a reproducible infinitesimal ignition mechanism; the
positive-curvature states provide the local oscillatory or saturated control.
The largest registered infinitesimal growth rate is
(\Gamma=0.456609006357168\).

This candidate predicts initiation.  It does not predict useful work,
localization, survivability, or termination.

## Candidate L-2: finite-volume mode selector

The periodic geometry admits an unstable nonhomogeneous coherence mode only
when an allowed wave number lies below the local tachyonic cutoff.  Among the
832 atlas states, the numbers supporting at least one such mode are:

| Periodic length | Supporting states | Largest unstable mode number |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 0 | 0 |
| 16 | 8 | 1 |
| 32 | 16 | 2 |
| 64 | 16 | 4 |

This is a candidate for selecting coherence scale by chamber size.  Periodic
mode exclusion is not evidence that a finite physical wall will impose the
same selection.

## Candidate L-3: isotropic coherence nucleation

The canonical scalar branch depends on the three-dimensional wavevector only
through \(|\boldsymbol{k}|^2\).  Numerical Fourier generators for axial,
planar-oblique, and fully three-dimensional diagonal perturbations agree with
that relation to (5.580536256551949\times10^{-13}) over three resolutions.

This is a candidate for direction-independent bulk nucleation.  It is not a
beam, vectorial push, or directional control effect.  Any such directionality
must arise from a background flow, vector target, boundary, or nonlinear
structure and must be qualified separately.
