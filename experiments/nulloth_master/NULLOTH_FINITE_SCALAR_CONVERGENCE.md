# The Measured Width of the First Finite Passage

## Synodic question

Campaign `NUL-BND-002` asks whether the first finite scalar passage survives
two tests that a single successful trajectory cannot answer: refinement of the
Cartesian patch and displacement of the causally irrelevant opposite wall.

Three one-dimensional runs use 12, 18, and 24 cells on the same unit patch.
A fourth run extends only the lower side of the domain while preserving the
fine run's spacing, pulse, upper boundary, and evolution time. The registered
observable is the fraction of initial scalar energy which has left the patch.

## Gates

The medium result must approach the fine result more closely than the coarse
result does. The equal-spacing enlarged patch must reproduce the fine loss
fraction within the declared tolerance. Homogeneous incoming scalar data,
opposite-edge amplitude, recovery, constitutive margins, scalar, Proca, and
CCZ4 constraints, carrier balance, and the signed finite entropy residual are
independent vetoes.

## Proscription

Convergence of this weak scalar passage does not complete the coupled boundary
symbol. It gives no leave for finite vector or material pulses, corners,
multiple dimensions, strong curvature, AMR, long-time reflection estimates,
or finite-domain spell promotion.
