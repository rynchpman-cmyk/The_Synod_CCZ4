# The First Finite Passage of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-BND-001` admits a narrow first passage from the periodic chamber.
On a one-dimensional Cartesian patch, the canonical production equations carry
a weak right-moving coherence pulse through the upper face while homogeneous
incoming scalar characteristic data remain unexcited. No corresponding signal
appears at the opposite face, so periodic wraparound is excluded.

The boundary uses the first-order scalar characteristics

\[
  U_{\rm out}=\Pi+\Phi_s,
  \qquad
  U_{\rm in}=\Pi-\Phi_s,
\]

where \(\Phi_s\) is the derivative along the outward spatial unit normal. The
outgoing right-hand side is retained and the incoming field is damped toward
homogeneous data. The scalar reduction gradient is evolved at the boundary;
it is not overwritten by the exact-gradient projection used in the periodic
backend.

## Accounting

The run uses the canonical discovery couplings, a dilute regular material
background, weak dynamical gravity, radiative CCZ4 data, and the existing
mixed-Proca boundary operator. Scalar energy leaves the patch, carrier-number
flux closes under the production RK weights, recovery remains admissible, and
the scalar, Proca, and CCZ4 constraint measures remain independently reported.

The finite material projection has a small signed entropy-balance residual.
This quantity is exposed as a veto diagnostic. It is not renamed as physical
entropy production and is not erased by the boundary implementation.
