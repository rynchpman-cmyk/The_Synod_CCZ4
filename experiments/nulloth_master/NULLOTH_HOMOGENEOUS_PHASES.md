# Homogeneous Nonlinear Phases of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

The canonical homogeneous coherence sector is a conservative quartic
double-well when the carrier drives (m_{\rm eff}^2<0).  It does not contain an
intrinsic settling mechanism.  The Synod therefore distinguishes the first
nonlinear turning amplitude from an asymptotic saturated state.

For homogeneous coherence,

\[
  E_\varphi=\frac12\Pi^2
  +\frac12m_{\rm eff}^2\varphi^2
  +\frac14\lambda_\varphi\varphi^4
\]

is conserved.  A seed placed in the linear growing eigenmode has
(\Pi_0=-\sqrt{-m_{\rm eff}^2}\varphi_0), and its first turning amplitude is
the positive root of

\[
  \frac12m_{\rm eff}^2\varphi_{\rm turn}^2
  +\frac14\lambda_\varphi\varphi_{\rm turn}^4=E_\varphi(0).
\]

Campaign `NUL-NLN-001` tests this relation over five activated carrier
densities and three seed amplitudes.  All fifteen trajectories turn over and
remain below the declared coherence limit.

## Phase transition

With the canonical discovery couplings and zero counterflow, activation begins
at

\[
  d_{\rm crit}=m_\varphi/\sqrt{g_d}=0.11547005383792516.
\]

Above this density the equilibria are

\[
  \varphi_\pm=\pm\sqrt{-m_{\rm eff}^2/\lambda_\varphi}.
\]

The two branches emerge continuously from zero.  Upward and downward density
sweeps coincide exactly in the registered equilibrium construction.  The
canonical quartic sector therefore exhibits a continuous symmetry-breaking
transition, two stable sign basins, and no first-order transition or
hysteresis.

Seeds with zero initial momentum and negative reduced energy remain in their
initial sign basin for at least 100 time units.  The exactly symmetric seed
remains on the basin boundary.  This lower bound is a conservative-sector
result: matter transport, radiation, boundaries, and gravity may change the
lifetime and must be tested separately.
