# The Synodic Charter of Permitted Control

## Pronouncement

The caster does not command a field.

The caster names a permitted outcome. We determine whether a physical path
reaches it, what must be moved, what must be spent, what must be heated, how
long causality requires, and how much reserve remains when the act is done.

Six channels are presently admitted: routing the Null, preconditioning
ordinary matter, driving the two vector sectors, seeding coherence through
initial or incoming data or through carrier-supported instability,
configuring physical interfaces, and allocating power and heat rejection.
Each channel acts through modeled initial data, incoming boundary data,
conserved flux, source current, or infrastructure state.

## What is not a control

We do not move the metric by decree. We do not spend lapse or shift. We do not
alter a coupling because a requested future lies beyond it. We do not call
constraint damping, constitutive projection, numerical entropy, or a raised
production cap an actuator. We do not add intention to a right-hand side.

These are not austerities of language. They are the boundary between solving
Nulloth and replacing it with obedience.

## The eight licensed questions

The present search grammar admits suppression, shielding, redirection,
confinement, transport, stabilization, sensing, and release. Each objective
must name an observable, an independently evolved control, the physical
channels it may use, and an abort surface. No favorable extremum is evidence
unless it survives conservation, entropy, constraints, recovery,
admissibility, resolution, and causal latency.

## The sixfold price

Every request is charged for energy, heat, carrier use, latency,
infrastructure, and admissibility reserve. Coordinate matter-plus-field energy
may be used where its discrete account is qualified; it is not global
gravitational energy. Physical heat is charged while numerical entropy remains
a convergence diagnostic. Carrier use includes both local peak inventory and
ship-wide routing burden. Latency includes preparation, propagation, growth,
feedback, and shutdown. Infrastructure includes damage and unavailable
redundancy. Admissibility is consumed as the trajectory approaches WATCH and
is exhausted at DENY.

The Synod may approve, weaken, delay, substitute, redirect, terminate, or deny.
The same rite may therefore receive a different answer in a different state.
This is not caprice. The state is part of the question.
