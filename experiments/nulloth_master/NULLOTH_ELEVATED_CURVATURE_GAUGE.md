# The Elevated-Curvature Gauge Trial of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-GRV-007` repeats the fine elevated-curvature trajectory under
harmonic and `1+log` slicing at gravitational coupling (0.5).  Scalar
amplitude, physical-metric deviation, and extrinsic-curvature norm are the
declared comparison observables.  Minimum lapse is the gauge-sensitive
control.

Agreement of the physical profile diagnostics is required alongside a
resolved change in the coordinate lapse.  CCZ4 and field constraints, number
conservation, entropy, constitutive convexity, and recovery remain independent
vetoes.

## Scope

This Article records empirical gauge robustness over a short elevated-
curvature horizon.  The coordinate norms are not gauge invariants, and the
trial does not establish long-time agreement, shift-gauge robustness,
stationarity, compactness, collapse, horizons, or radiation.
