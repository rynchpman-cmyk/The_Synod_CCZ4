# The Faces and Voices of the Finite Gate

## Synodic derivation

Campaign `NUL-BND-007` derives state-local incoming, glancing, and outgoing
spectral projectors for the 48-variable constrained CCZ4-plus-Nulloth
principal system. Repeated and glancing modes are represented by clustered
SVD eigenspaces; naïve individual eigenvectors are forbidden at degeneracy.

At a generic audited state each face carries 21 incoming, 6 glancing, and 21
outgoing modes. The discovery-box face survey has three signatures because
material currents can become tangent to a face. Incoming count therefore
ranges from 19 to 21 and the glancing count reaches 10. Boundary data must be
selected from the local projector; a fixed mode count is not generally valid.

## Implementation judgment

The present finite backend has exact characteristic prescriptions for two
incoming modes: one scalar field and one Z4 constraint field. Fifteen further
incoming modes receive componentwise radiative treatment across the remaining
CCZ4 and two Proca sectors. Up to four material modes receive outgoing-only
finite-volume fluxes rather than data from the full coupled projector.

Thus the incoming inventory and projectors are derived, but the complete
coupled absorbing boundary is not qualified. No Synodic Article may infer
complete boundary control from the successful scalar, vector, or material
passage campaigns alone.
