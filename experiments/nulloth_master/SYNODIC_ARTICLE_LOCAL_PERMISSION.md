# Synodic Article: The Bounds of Local Permission

## Pronouncement

We do not call a state possible because its numbers are finite.

We call it possible only while its currents admit recovery, its constitutive
maps retain rank, its characteristic families remain complete, its physical
signals remain causal, and its geometry can carry the state without surrender
of constraint.

This distinction is the first law of casting:

> **An effect outside permission is not a stronger spell. It is the loss of a
> world in which the spell could be asked.**

The following bounds govern the present discovery domain of Nulloth. They are
not laws for all conceivable parameter choices. They are the territory we
have resolved and are therefore willing to preserve.

## I. The resolved voice of the system

The local physical symbol contains twenty-five fields: the two material
currents, the coherence field and its directional reduction, and both
Proca-cleaning sectors. The algebraically reduced CCZ4 symbol adds twenty-three
gravitational, gauge, and constraint variables. Together they form a
forty-eight-variable characteristic basis.

The basis is complete in the audited composition. Its measured rank is
forty-eight, its condition is `33.927390485351204`, and its largest eigenspace
residual is `7.637207550152257e-11`. Matter sources and metric-connection terms
remain present in finite-wavelength evolution, but their normalized cross
blocks decay with measured order `0.9968909794934206`. They vanish from the
high-frequency principal limit because Nulloth is minimally coupled.

The Gamma-driver carries a coordinate gauge speed of
`1.1547005387263132`. This is not a material signal and is not permission for
superluminal action. The maximum physical Nulloth speed in the qualified
domain is one.

Seal: **QUALIFIED**, NUL-CHR-002.

## VI. The vectors do not rewrite the cone

Proca masses, kinetic normalizations, mass mixing, target charge, and target
current are algebraic in the principal problem. Across the audited parameter
sets, the fourteen-variable doubled Proca block and its spectrum changed by
exactly zero. Numerical sixteen-field Jacobians differed by at most
`4.533062814005007e-11` across longitudinal, transverse, and oblique target
currents.

Orientation still changes force. Longitudinal electric current receives power;
exact transverse electric current receives none. This is lower-order dynamics,
not a deformation of causal permission.

## VII. The guard at the threshold

The runtime guard speaks in three words:

- **SAFE**: all sampled hard and warning gates retain reserve;
- **WATCH**: the state remains admissible but approaches a declared margin;
- **DENY**: a production cap, convexity gate, complete-eigenspace gate, or
  physical causal gate has failed.

The guard transforms coordinate velocities into a local orthonormal frame. It
does not confuse a curved coordinate component with physical speed. It samples
up to sixteen cells and eighteen directions per cell by default. It diagnoses
and may halt; it never changes an evolved field to manufacture permission.

The registered guard correctly classified the safe interior, the amplitude
watch state, hard amplitude denial, the independent low-carrier superluminal
denial, and a real production recovery state. Its curved-frame speed invariant
agreed to `2.168404344971009e-19`.

Seal: **QUALIFIED**, NUL-CHR-008.

## VIII. What remains unknown

We have not proven a global admissible domain.

We have not closed the broad coupling boundary into a certified
multidimensional mesh. We have not shown that finite boundaries, shocks,
strong curvature, or every nonlinear trajectory remain inside these local
surfaces. We have not licensed a spell merely because its initial state is
permitted.

Local permission is necessary. It is not promotion.

A candidate spell must carry this audit along its entire trajectory. If it
approaches WATCH, the Synod may reduce, reroute, or terminate the control. If
it enters DENY, the requested future is refused.

> **The Synod does not punish the impossible. The Synod declines to evolve it.**
