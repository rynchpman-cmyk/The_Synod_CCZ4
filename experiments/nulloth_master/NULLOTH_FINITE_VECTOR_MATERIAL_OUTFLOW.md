# The Second and Third Finite Passages of Nulloth

## Synodic trial

Campaign `NUL-BND-003` places independent transverse-vector and co-moving
material pulses beside the already observed scalar passage. Each active case
is compared with a separately evolved finite-patch control, so the small
expansion of the dilute background is removed from the declared observable.

The vector seed is a transverse \(A_y\) Gaussian with conjugate momentum
chosen for rightward propagation. Its retained quadratic field norm is
measured after subtracting the control fields. The material seed jointly
loads ordinary and carrier density and gives both currents the same localized
rightward velocity. Its excess baryon and carrier numbers are measured
against the control before and after evolution.

## Vetoes and scope

Both pulses must lose nonzero content through the upper face without a signal
at the opposite edge. CCZ4, Proca, scalar-reduction, recovery, constitutive,
carrier-balance, and finite entropy-account diagnostics remain independent
vetoes.

This is a one-resolution passage trial. It does not qualify reflection
coefficients, complete coupled characteristics, multiple dimensions, shocks,
strong gravity, AMR, or finite-domain spell use.
