# The Short-Horizon Gauge Trial of Nulloth

## Synodic finding

Campaign `NUL-GRV-002` repeats the weak-field active-versus-scalar-free trial
under harmonic and `1+log` slicing.  Shift evolution is disabled in both
cases.  Each member begins from its own periodic CMC constraint solve and is
advanced through one quarter time unit by the complete production solver.

The final coherence amplitude and active-versus-control physical-metric
contrast agree closely between slicings.  The lapse contrast, as a coordinate
gauge field, changes substantially.  This separation is the intended test:
agreement is required of the declared short-horizon matter and backreaction
observables, not of the gauge variable itself.

## Vetoes and scope

Number conservation, complete-step entropy, CCZ4 and field constraints,
constitutive convexity, and recovery remain independent vetoes.  Passing this
trial establishes short-horizon robustness only at one coarse resolution and
one weak gravitational coupling.  It does not establish gauge invariance,
long-duration agreement, radiation robustness, moving-puncture behavior, or
strong-field validity.
