# Cartesian Physical-Normal MOTS Calibration

## Outcome

The corrected Cartesian observer is implemented and connected to dynamical
CCZ4 snapshots. It evaluates the surface geometry parametrically instead of
differentiating a volume extension of the level-set normal. The analytic
flat-space gate is exact to roundoff, and the coincident-puncture
Schwarzschild control converges toward the direct answer.

The calibration also sets an important production boundary: a strongly
distorted common horizon represented by only roughly six Cartesian cells per
mean coordinate radius is not yet in a clean asymptotic regime. Its principal
mode must be interpreted through a resolution ensemble, especially near the
branch fold. Attaching the observer is now safe; treating every single-grid
zero crossing as a continuum prediction is not.

## What Changed

`ParametricAxisymmetricMOTSFinder` uses an axisymmetric radial embedding

\[
X^i(\theta,\phi)=c^i+r(\theta)e_r^i
\]

and computes its tangents and second derivatives analytically. At the angular
surface points it samples only the physical spatial metric \(\gamma_{ij}\),
physical extrinsic curvature \(K_{ij}\), and Cartesian Christoffel symbols
\(\Gamma^i{}_{jk}\). From these it constructs the induced two-metric,
outward physical unit normal, mean curvature, area measure, and

\[
\Theta_+=D_i s^i+K_{ij}s^is^j-K.
\]

The physical displacement generated by every log-radius coefficient is
computed from the same surface normal. The existing induced-area,
physical-normal stability operator therefore no longer depends on the old
volume level-set normalization.

## Exact and Controlled Gates

For a unit sphere in Cartesian Minkowski data, the corrected observer returns
\(\Theta_+=2\) pointwise, \(A=4\pi\), and unit log-radius-to-normal
displacement to approximately \(2\times10^{-13}\) or better.

For the coincident-puncture Schwarzschild surface, the direct oracle has
\(\lambda_0=0.25\). With a length-6 box, the corrected Cartesian values are:

| Resolution | Spacing | RMS expansion | Corrected \(\lambda_0\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 48 | 0.1250 | 5.866e-2 | 0.166120 |
| 64 | 0.09375 | 1.718e-2 | 0.220086 |
| 80 | 0.0750 | 7.711e-3 | 0.237581 |
| 96 | 0.0625 | 4.416e-3 | 0.248216 |

At \(N=96\), the principal eigenvalue error is about 0.7%.

## Distorted Outer/Common Branch

Each Cartesian observer measured the *same fixed surface* supplied by a direct
angular solve. No Cartesian surface was re-optimized, so the table isolates
observer error from branch-finding error.

| Separation | Direct \(\lambda_0\) | Legacy N=96 | Corrected N=96 | Corrected RMS expansion |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.750 | 0.098658 | 0.125200 | 0.067630 | 1.930e-2 |
| 0.765 | 0.014467 | -0.015668 | 0.017265 | 3.405e-2 |

The corrected observer preserves the near-fold sign at \(d=0.765\), while the
legacy volume-level-set observer gives the wrong sign. At \(d=0.750\), neither
observer uniformly dominates: the corrected absolute eigenvalue error is
about 0.0310 and the legacy error about 0.0265. This is evidence of remaining
Cartesian differentiation/interpolation and puncture-phase error, not grounds
for selecting whichever estimator happens to be closer in one row.

The two-step finite-difference audit of the normal operator remains at roughly
parts in \(10^9\). The dominant uncertainty is the Cartesian geometry supplied
to the surface, not the coefficient perturbation step.

## Branch-Selection Warning

A coarse continuation jump from \(d=0\) directly to \(d=0.75\) converged to
the inner common branch and produced the opposite stability sign. The final
calibration seeds the outer/common branch independently at each requested
separation. Dynamical campaigns should retain explicit names such as
`common_outer` and `common_inner`; a generic `common` label is insufficient
near a fold.

## Attach to production33 CCZ4 Snapshots

The observer remains outside the evolution right-hand side:

```python
from causal_repair.kkt_horizon import (
    CCZ4PhysicalNormalMOTSTracker,
    MOTSBranchDefinition,
    ParametricAxisymmetricMOTSFinder,
    SpectralMOTSParameters,
)

parameters = SpectralMOTSParameters(
    modes=((0, 0), (2, 0), (4, 0), (6, 0), (8, 0)),
    n_theta=32,
    n_phi=16,
    minimum_radius=0.05,
    maximum_radius=2.0,
    maximum_mode_amplitude=2.0,
)
finder = ParametricAxisymmetricMOTSFinder(solver.grid, parameters)
tracker = CCZ4PhysicalNormalMOTSTracker(
    solver.ccz4,
    finder,
    [
        MOTSBranchDefinition("common_outer", (0.0, 0.0, 0.0), 0.65),
        MOTSBranchDefinition("common_inner", (0.0, 0.0, 0.0), 0.40),
    ],
)

# state.geometry is a CCZ4State in production33.
observations = tracker.observe(state.geometry)
record = {
    name: observation.diagnostics()
    for name, observation in observations.items()
}
observer_checkpoint = tracker.checkpoint()
```

Restore `observer_checkpoint` alongside the evolution checkpoint before an
append run. For expensive production evolutions, observe saved snapshots or a
cadenced subset of live steps rather than every Runge--Kutta stage.
