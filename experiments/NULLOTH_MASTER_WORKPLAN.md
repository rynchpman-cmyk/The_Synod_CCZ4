# Nulloth Master Spec Workplan

Status: active  
Canonical prose name: **Nulloth**  
Scientific authority: reproducible solver evidence  
Narrative authority: the Synod

This is the persistent checklist for mapping Nulloth and producing the Synod's
Master Spec. A checked item means that the named artifact exists and has passed
its stated verification; it does not imply validity outside the declared
domain.

Human-facing prose uses **Nulloth** without a version number. Machine formats
may retain compatibility versions, such as checkpoint schema identifiers.

## Operating law

Every scientific campaign follows this sequence:

- [ ] State the question.
- [ ] Register the prediction before the decisive run.
- [ ] Define the falsifier and decisive observable.
- [ ] Audit the local mathematics and admissibility conditions.
- [ ] Run controls and the minimum useful resolution ladder.
- [ ] Measure convergence, conservation, constraints, and characteristic health.
- [ ] Attempt to falsify the apparent result with perturbed data.
- [ ] Classify the resulting claim in the Evidence Ledger.
- [ ] Write or update the corresponding Synodic Article.
- [ ] Record the unmapped domain and denial conditions.

## Evidence seals

- **POSTULATED**: selected as part of the seed action or formulation.
- **DERIVED**: established analytically from stated postulates.
- **REGRESSED**: reproduced a required limiting theory.
- **OBSERVED**: measured numerically but not fully qualified.
- **QUALIFIED**: reproducible inside an explicit sampled domain.
- **PROMOTED**: supports an independently controllable spell protocol.
- **PROSCRIBED**: known to violate a required physical or numerical condition.
- **UNKNOWN**: deliberately unmapped or not yet resolved.
- **REVOKED**: superseded or falsified by stronger evidence.
