## 1. OBJECTIVE

Investigate a candidate semisimple characteristic degeneracy discovered during an independent SciPy/mpmath analysis of the Physical Nulloth master action.

The longitudinal Hadamard instability region appears to close at a single point in constitutive state space before reopening on either side.

The objectives are:

- Independently reproduce the pinch using the native Nulloth implementation.
- Establish its algebraic and geometric eigenvalue multiplicities.
- Determine whether the full coupled PDE remains strongly hyperbolic at the pinch.
- Investigate arbitrary propagation directions near the pinch.
- Determine whether autonomous or controlled dynamical evolution can transit the pinch without losing admissibility.
- Search for additional unexpected phenomena in its immediate neighborhood.

Treat all reference results below as hypotheses to reproduce, not assertions that the production implementation must be modified to satisfy.

Preserve the existing solver architecture and qualification policies. Do not silently modify constitutive physics, disable recovery gates, clip characteristic eigenvalues, or introduce artificial stabilization.
